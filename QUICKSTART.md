## 📋 What's Included

### ✨ 5 Fully Functional Modules:

1. **Score Predictor** (`/score-predictor`)
   - Predict match outcomes
   - Win percentage calculator
   - Run rate analysis

2. **Shot Analyzer** (`/shot-analyzer`)
   - 8 cricket shots with detailed tips
   - Difficulty ratings
   - Expert guidance

3. **Match Simulator** (`/match-simulator`)
   - Interactive toss
   - Ball-by-ball simulation
   - Live scoreboard

4. **Scorecard System** (`/scorecard`)
   - Add batsmen & bowlers
   - Ball-by-ball entry
   - Auto-calculated stats
   - LocalStorage persistence

5. **Cricket Quiz** (`/quiz`)
   - 10 challenging questions
   - Instant feedback
   - Score tracking
